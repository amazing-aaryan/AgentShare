# Code of Conduct

AgentShare contributors must keep project spaces respectful, constructive, and
focused on the work. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted
personal attention, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban
participants whose conduct is harmful to the project or its community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through their GitHub
profiles. Do not use public issues to report sensitive personal information.
Maintainers will review reports in confidence and respond proportionately.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing AgentShare in
public spaces.
